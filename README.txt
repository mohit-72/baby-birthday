1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder.

The site is made without personal photos and runs locally. No hosting/account is required on your computer.

PRIVACY:
- It does not upload your messages or photos anywhere.
- style.css imports Google Fonts for a nicer look. To avoid that external request and keep the page strictly offline, delete the first @import line from style.css. It will use system fonts.

CUSTOMIZE:
- Edit text in index.html.
- Birthday date: October 26, 2026.

This is a local website. If you upload it to public hosting, anyone with access to the site may view its contents. Keep it local if privacy matters most.
